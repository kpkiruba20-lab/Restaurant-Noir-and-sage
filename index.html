Our Story

Quiet room. Loud flavour.

NOIR & SAGE opened in 2018 after Chef Arjun Menon returned to Chennai from a decade in the kitchens of London and Copenhagen. He wanted a restaurant that felt like a private dinner, where the cooking spoke softly but left a lasting impression.

Our philosophy is simple: cook what the season offers. Curry leaf from a Mahabalipuram farm, line-caught fish from Kovalam, mushrooms foraged in the Nilgiris. Fire, smoke and patience do the rest.

Inside, expect low amber light, dark walnut, linen and a soundtrack of soft jazz. It is a room made for anniversaries, long conversations and unhurried evenings.
